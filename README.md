# 🛡️ GigShield AI

> **Parametric income protection for India's gig workforce — automatic, instant, and built around how delivery partners actually earn.**

---

## 🌧️ The Problem

India's gig economy is the backbone of our digital infrastructure — yet delivery partners lose **20–30% of their weekly earnings** due to uncontrollable weather and social disruptions. Traditional insurance ignores this gap entirely.

GigShield AI exists to change that.

---

## 🚀 What It Does

GigShield AI is a **parametric insurance platform** that automatically triggers payouts for lost income when external disruptions occur — including:

- 🌧️ Extreme rainfall events
- 🌡️ Dangerous heat conditions
- 🚫 Curfews and civil disruptions

Payouts are triggered without manual claims. The moment a verified disruption crosses a defined threshold, workers receive compensation.

The platform operates on a **Weekly Pricing Model**, deliberately designed to align with the gig worker's earnings cycle — not monthly billing cycles built for salaried employees.

---

## ⚙️ How It's Built

| Layer | Technology |
|---|---|
| Backend | Java (Spring Boot) — core policy logic & parametric trigger monitoring |
| Weather Data | OpenWeatherMap API — hyper-local rainfall & temperature tracking |
| Claims | Zero-Touch automation — payouts trigger the moment disruptions are verified |

### Key Design Decisions

**Zero-Touch Claims:** The system eliminates manual claims processing entirely. An AI-driven pipeline monitors conditions in real time and initiates payouts automatically — respecting the worker's time.

**Parametric Triggers:** Thresholds are carefully calibrated to distinguish between inconvenient drizzle and work-halting disruptions. This required deep research into delivery partner personas and real-world conditions across Indian cities.

**The Golden Rule:** GigShield AI strictly excludes health coverage and vehicle repairs. The platform focuses 100% on **income protection** — doing one thing well rather than many things poorly.

---

## 📐 Weekly Premium Model

Premiums are priced and billed weekly to mirror how gig workers earn and budget. This model was designed to remain both **affordable for workers** and **sustainable** for the platform over time.

## 💡 What We Learned

> *"Move away from manual claims to an AI-driven system that respects the worker's time."*

The biggest insight was the power of **Parametric Automation** — shifting from a claims-review model (which fails gig workers who can't afford to wait) to a verification-and-trigger model that pays out at the speed of disruption.
